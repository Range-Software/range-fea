## Using the 3D area with a MacBook trackpad

- **Clear all picks:** hold **⌘ Command** and perform a two-finger click (secondary click). Secondary click must be enabled in macOS Trackpad settings.

**Escape does not currently clear picks.** Clicking empty space does not clear them either. Picks in the 3D area are separate from row selections in the Model tree.

On Windows and Linux, use **Ctrl** instead of **⌘ Command**, **Alt** instead of **⌥ Option**, and **Ctrl + right-click** to clear all picks.
